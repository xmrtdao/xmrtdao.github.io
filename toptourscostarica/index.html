About Tayra Tours

We are a family-owned tour operator based in La Fortuna, Costa Rica. For over 15 years, we've been showing visitors the magic of Arenal Volcano National Park and the surrounding rainforest.

Our name comes from the tayra, a native Costa Rican mammal known for its agility and curiosity - qualities we bring to every adventure we lead.
